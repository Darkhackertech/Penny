About Penny

Penny is designed to provide a simple platform where users can participate in available earning activities, track their rewards and manage their account.

What is Penny?

Penny is an online rewards platform built around tasks, activities and referral opportunities. Users can create an account, explore available opportunities and track approved rewards from within their Penny account.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make the experience simple and easy to understand. Penny is designed so that users can clearly see available activities, their account information and their transaction history.

What You Can Do on Penny

Complete Tasks

Explore available tasks and follow the requirements provided for each activity.

Track Earnings

Keep track of approved rewards and your account transaction history.

Refer Others

Use your referral tools to invite other people to the platform.

Important Information

Available earning activities, reward amounts, eligibility requirements and withdrawal conditions may vary. Always review the requirements displayed on Penny before participating in an activity.
