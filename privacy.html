Privacy Policy

This page explains the types of information Penny may collect, how it may be used and how users can protect their information.

Last Updated: September 2026

Penny is designed to respect user privacy. Please read this policy carefully so you understand how information may be handled when using the platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Penny may collect information that you provide when creating an account, contacting support or using platform features.

  • Full name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Contact information
  • Account information
  • Information submitted through support forms
  • Activity and transaction information

2. Information You Provide

Information such as your name, email address or other details may be provided directly by you when you register, update your account or contact Penny support.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Providing requested platform features.
  • Processing account-related requests.
  • Responding to support messages.
  • Maintaining platform security.
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity.
  • Improving the Penny experience.

4. Contact Information

If you use the Penny contact form, the information you enter may be included in the WhatsApp message prepared for Penny support.

You should avoid submitting passwords, PINs, bank passwords, verification codes or other highly sensitive credentials through a general contact form.

5. Account Information

Account information may be used to identify your account, provide account features and maintain the relationship between you and the platform.

6. Activity and Transaction Information

If Penny provides earning activities, transaction records or withdrawal features, information related to those activities may be associated with your account.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Penny may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage for functions such as remembering preferences, maintaining temporary session information or improving the user experience.

Browser-based storage should not be treated as a secure replacement for a properly protected server-side account system.

8. Security

Reasonable security measures should be used to protect information handled by the platform. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Sharing of Information

Personal information should only be shared when necessary for legitimate platform operations, support, security, legal requirements or other purposes described to the user.

10. Third-Party Services

Penny may use third-party services in the future for features such as authentication, hosting, analytics, payment processing or communications. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Users should only use Penny where they are legally permitted to do so. If age restrictions apply to a particular feature, users must meet those requirements before using it.

12. Your Information

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Penny's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question or concern, please contact Penny support through the contact page.

Have a Privacy Question?

Contact Penny support if you need clarification about information handled through the platform.
