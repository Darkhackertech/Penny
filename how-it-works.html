How Penny Works

Penny is designed to make it simple to discover available activities, complete eligible tasks and keep track of approved rewards from your account.

Getting Started With Penny

Follow these steps to understand the basic Penny experience.

1

Create Your Account

Register for a Penny account by providing the information required during registration. Make sure the information you provide is accurate.

2

Explore Opportunities

After signing in, you can view the activities and opportunities that are available to your account.

3

Complete Tasks

Select an available activity and follow its instructions. Some activities may have specific eligibility or completion requirements.

4

Wait for Approval

Completed activities may need to be reviewed before a reward is approved and added to your account.

5

Track Your Earnings

Your Penny account can be used to view approved rewards, transactions and other account information.

6

Request Withdrawal

When your account meets the applicable withdrawal requirements, you can request a withdrawal using the available withdrawal options.

Before You Start

Available activities, reward amounts, eligibility requirements, review periods and withdrawal conditions may vary.

Completing an activity does not necessarily mean that a reward will be approved. Activities may be reviewed according to their individual requirements.

Penny should not be presented as a guarantee of income. Always review the requirements of an activity before completing it.
